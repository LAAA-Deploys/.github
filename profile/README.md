# LAAA-Deploys

Deploy repositories for the LAAA Team of Marcus & Millichap, a Los Angeles apartment investment sales team ([laaa.com](https://www.laaa.com)).

## What lives here

- **Deal websites:** one repository per property website, served at `{slug}.laaa.com`. Broker opinion of value sites are named `{slug}-bov`; offering memorandum sites are named `{slug}-om`.
- **apto-hub:** the team's CRM procedures and connector (private).
- **laaa-comps:** the team's comps platform at comps.laaa.com (private).
- A few small team sites.

## Instructions

Deal website instructions live in `gscher1311/LAAA-AI-Prompts` (`reporting/bov_workflow.md`). CRM instructions live in `AGENTS.md` in `apto-hub`. Both are private to the team.
